# ItemSize

**Make items bigger or smaller, exactly how you like it.**

ItemSize lets you change the size of every item in three places: in your **hand**, on the **ground** and in your **inventory**. A clean menu with a live preview makes it quick and simple.

## Features

- **Per item:** each item has its own size, from 10 % to 300 %.
- **Three views:** Ground, Hand (first and third person) and Inventory.
- **Live preview:** see the result while you drag the slider.
- **Simple controls:** slider, - / + buttons, mouse wheel, arrow keys, presets or type a percentage.
- **All views switch:** change all three views at once or each one on its own.
- **Search and filters:** find items by name or ID, filter by Block, Tool, Armor or edited items.
- **Works with modded items.**
- **Saved automatically.** Global ON / OFF switch and reset buttons included.

## How to use

Press **O** in a world, pick an item, set the size. That's it. You can change the key in the controls settings.

## Good to know

- Client-side only: nobody else sees your sizes, and no server needs the mod.
- Weapons and ammunition are intentionally not included.
- Requires **Fabric API**.
